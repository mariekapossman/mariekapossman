Marieka Possman (b. Tokyo, Japan) works in sculpture, using metaphor as a site of relation where stories and substances press into contact to generate new forms of meaning. They believe in metaphor as a way of approaching a subject without seizing it, generating a space of sensuous closeness through symbolic distance. They are based in Providence, RI.
